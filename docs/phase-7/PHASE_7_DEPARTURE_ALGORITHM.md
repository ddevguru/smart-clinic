# Phase 7 — Departure Recommendation Algorithm
## Formula, Edge Cases & Recalibration Engine

### 1. The Core Formula
The exact recommended time for a patient to leave their location is defined by:

$$\text{recommended\_departure\_at} = \text{expected\_turn\_at} - \text{traffic\_duration\_seconds} - \text{arrival\_buffer\_seconds}$$

Where:
- $\text{expected\_turn\_at}$: The UTC timestamp generated by the Queue Intelligence Engine.
- $\text{traffic\_duration\_seconds}$: The driving duration retrieved from Google Maps Routes API (or cached snapshot).
- $\text{arrival\_buffer\_seconds}$: Clinic reception arrival buffer (configurable between 10 and 15 minutes, default: 600s).

### 2. Arrival Buffer Philosophy
In the Indian outpatient setting, patients arriving at the exact minute their token is called causes OPD workflow pauses (e.g. taking vitals, confirming payment, elevator delays). 
- An arrival buffer of 10–15 minutes ensures the patient is comfortably seated in the waiting area before their token is summoned to the doctor's desk.

### 3. Edge Case Handling
1. **Patient is already late**:
   - If $\text{recommended\_departure\_at} < \text{NOW()}$, the status is flagged as `LATE` or `LEAVE NOW`.
   - The UI warns the patient: "Time to leave immediately — your expected turn is in $X$ minutes".
2. **Doctor is delayed**:
   - If doctor reports a delay of $+20$ minutes, $\text{expected\_turn\_at}$ is pushed back by 1200 seconds.
   - $\text{recommended\_departure\_at}$ is pushed back identically, allowing the patient to remain comfortably at home or office rather than waiting in an overcrowded clinic.
3. **Queue moves faster than expected**:
   - If multiple patients ahead cancel or don't show up, recalibration advances $\text{expected\_turn\_at}$ and sends an expedited "Turn Approaching Faster" alert.
